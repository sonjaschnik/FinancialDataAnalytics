Golf VII Variant (2013–2020)

Archive

Superior practicality

Factory code: Type AUV

With the Golf VII Variant, Volkswagen presents the estate version of one of the most successful vehicle model series in the world from spring 2013. The Golf VII Variant redefines the compact estate car segment. It is technically sophisticated, versatile, and sets new benchmarks in its class in many relevant disciplines from space and efficiency to driving dynamics.

The design follows the clear design language of the Golf VII but has its own characteristics with its extended body and rear end with elegant new proportions. Flowing lines, precise shoulders, and a harmonious combination of function and form convey a feeling of enduring solidity. Despite the increased space, the Variant seems sportier than its predecessor, aerodynamic fine tuning is rewarded with an optimum drag coefficient value of just 0.27 in ideal conditions.

In the interior, the Golf Variant transforms the standards of higher vehicle classes into the compact segment: high-quality materials and carefully considered ergonomics characterise the cockpit. Frequent drivers swear by the superb ergonomic seats. Compared with the predecessor, the boot capacity of the five-seater increases considerably to 605 litres with an upright backrest and up to 1,620 litres with the rear seats folded down. Depending on the version, the permitted towing capacity is a maximum of two tonnes (up to 12 percent gradient).

For the first time, alongside the classic Variant versions there are also new derivatives with a stronger focus on the Lifestyle aspect. The Golf R Variant offers driving performance at sports car level, it reaches speeds of up to 270 km/h. The Golf Variant Alltrack optimised for easy terrain picks up on the SUV trend with increased ground clearance, off-road chassis, and off-road mode.

This sees the Golf VII Variant offer a new dimension of diversity: it is a family car, sports car, company car, and leisure vehicle all rolled into one – a universal companion. Its technical substance, comfort features, and workmanship significantly surpass the compact class level.

Golf VII Variant model update

2014/2015

Series addition

The Variant family becomes sportier and even more diverse: the dynamic GTD Variant with a powerful 2.0 TDI and 184 PS debuts in 2015. A real achiever is the R Variant with 2.0 TSI engine, 300 PS, and 4 MOTION (all-wheel drive). Both derivatives combine excellent utility with significantly increased dynamics while remaining astonishingly economical. As a version with slight off-road capability, the Alltrack version with more ground clearance and all-wheel drive adds an interesting SUV alternative.

2015

Extras but not a diva

The new Allstar special edition significantly enhances standard comfort on board as of December 2016. The Allstar has 16-inch Linas alloy wheels in black with a diamond-cut surface, multifunction leather steering wheel, MFA Premium, cruise control, Climatronic, radio composition colour, tinted rear windows, mirror package, and fog lights. Other highlights are the special colour Carbon Steel Grey, aluminium pedals, and the Pentastripe seat covers. For peace of mind, the warranty is extended by three years for up to 50,000 kilometres. The Allstar Plus package comes with 17-inch Singapore alloy wheels, bi-xenon headlights with LED daytime running light and Dynamic Light Assist, as well as LED taillights and the Lights & Vision package.

2016

Sounds good

The Sound special edition enhances the comfort line with 16-inch Woodstock alloy wheels, fog lights, “SOUND” stickers on the B-pillar or mudguards, door sills with SOUND lettering, seat materials with the Equalizer promotional design featuring contrasting stitching, additional chrome elements, Climatronic, ParkPilot, winter package, heated front seats, as well as tinted rear side windows and a tinted rear windscreen.

2017

Successful update

A major model upgrade furnishes the Variant, like the Golf Limousine, with more modern infotainment and assistance technology: Highlights are the new Discover Pro infotainment system with gesture control, the Active Info Display as a fully digital instrument cluster, and practical new assistance systems (including congestion assistance and Trailer Assist). An innovation under the bonnet is the new 1.5 TSI Evo engine with ACT technology and a 7-speed DSG generation.

2018

Get involved

The new Join special edition pulls up in December. On board are essential extras such as the Discover Media infotainment system including Car-Net Guide & Inform, Woodstock 6.5J x 16 alloy wheels, fog lights, winter package, and Front Assist. The special colour Blue Dusk Metallic and the Join Plus package with Madrid 7J x 17 alloy wheels, Active Lighting System, and rain sensors are also available to order.

2019

Cleverer driving

The IQ.Drive special edition showcases the current driver assistance systems offered by the Modular Transverse Toolkit (MQB). 16-inch Dublin alloy wheels, Cushion fabric seats (black in the central section, Petrol at the sides), ACC system, Lights and Vision package, Blindspot and Lane Assist, parking assistant, Climatronic, winter package, and the IQ.DRIVE logo projection onto the front doors all come as standard. Options for the special edition include the IQ.DRIVE Plus package with 17-inch Madrid alloy wheels, Discover Media infotainment system, App Connect, Medial Control, voice control, and traffic sign recognition.

2019/2020

End of production

The presentation of the eighth-generation Golf starts the model change. The series is gradually discontinued starting in 2019. The world premiere of the Golf VIII Variant as the successor comes in September 2020.
